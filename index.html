What AtvTube is

AtvTube is an independently developed application for supported Android TVs and TV boxes. It focuses on everyday convenience on a television screen—such as remote-friendly controls, on-screen local time, and easier use of the device’s preferred subtitle language when those options are available in the app.

AtvTube does not sell YouTube subscriptions, video content, or access to restricted videos. Availability of any on-screen feature depends on your device and the apps you use.

What you get for free

  • Download and install the APK from this site
  • Use AtvTube’s available features on a compatible device
  • No account required on this website to download

What a purchaser receives

  • A one-time device license for this device
  • Digital delivery: activate on the TV after payment (no physical shipping)
  • The device is marked as licensed and the periodic authorization reminder stops
  • App features are not held behind a separate paywall; the license is about device authorization and the reminder
