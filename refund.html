Refund Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · AtvTube one-time device license

1. What you are buying

You are purchasing a digital software license that activates your device for licensing purposes and stops the optional on-screen authorization reminder. There is no physical shipment. Delivery is electronic after successful payment.

2. When a refund may be available

You may request a refund within 7 days of purchase if:

  • the license could not be activated on your device after you contacted support and we could not resolve the issue within a reasonable time; or
  • you were charged in error (for example a clear duplicate charge); or
  • a refund is required by applicable law or by our payment partner (Creem).

3. When a refund is generally not available

Except where required by law or by Creem, purchases are generally non-refundable if:

  • the license has already been successfully activated on a device; or
  • you changed your mind after successful activation; or
  • the issue is due to unsupported devices, incorrect installation, or factors outside our control after the product was delivered as described.

4. How to request a refund

Email tebugpt@gmail.com with:

  • the email used for the purchase (if any)
  • approximate purchase date and amount
  • order / receipt / checkout reference if available
  • a short description of the problem

We aim to respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are processed through Creem to the original payment method; timing depends on Creem and your bank or card issuer.

5. Payment partner

Payments are processed by Creem (Merchant of Record). Creem may apply its own procedures for refunds and chargebacks. Please contact us first so we can help before opening a dispute with your card issuer.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page at the time of purchase applies to that purchase.
